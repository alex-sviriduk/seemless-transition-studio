# Music credits

SeemLess includes 22 approximately 30-second MP3 excerpts from [FMA small](https://github.com/mdeff/fma), assembled by Michaël Defferrard, Kirell Benzi, Pierre Vandergheynst, and Xavier Bresson (ISMIR 2017). Audio and license records were retrieved from the project's published archives on September 24, 2026.

Each recording remains under its individual Creative Commons Attribution license below, separate from the application code. The bundled MP3 bytes are unchanged from FMA small; that dataset excerpts the original recordings. Playback is downmixed/resampled to 22.05 kHz mono, and users can trim and crossfade excerpts. Artist links do not imply endorsement. FMA source pages may have moved since the dataset was published.
